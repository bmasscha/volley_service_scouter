Simple volleyball service scouting app is a tool to scout the service of the team on an android tablet

What is needs to do:

- before the game starts we have to enter the team name and the player numbers of the team.
- when player x is at the service, we have to be able to draw the trajectory on a volleyball court image
- the trajectory has a start position and an end position
- we want to enter the type of serve (topspin, jump float, float, jump topspin)
- we also want to see all previous trajectories on the court image for that player
- if the service is ace the color of the trajectory is green
- if the service is a fault the color of the trajectory is red
- if the service is in and not an ace the color of the trajectory is blue
- we want to save all trajectories for all players in a csv file for that game

The goal is to predict the next trajectory based on the previous trajectories and the type of serve.
We want to predict the probability that the service is an ace, a fault or in and not an ace.
